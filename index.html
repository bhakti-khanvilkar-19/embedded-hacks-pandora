How this notebook works

Every piece of knowledge is classified before it's written down: domain → category → topic. SPI and I2C are separate topics under Protocols; TCP, IP, and UDP are separated under Networking; Linux, bootloaders, and Snap each evolve independently. Nothing gets dumped into a catch-all folder. See GUIDE.md for the full convention on where new material belongs and how to add it.
