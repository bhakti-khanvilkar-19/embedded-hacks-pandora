Domain

Electronics

Every other domain in this notebook eventually rests on a handful of circuit-level ideas: what voltage, current, and resistance actually are; how resistors, capacitors, and inductors behave; how transistors switch; and how the analog world gets turned into numbers a microcontroller can use. This is that foundation — kept separate from Sensors (specific sensing elements) and Embedded (the microcontroller itself) because it's the layer underneath both. It also covers the computer-hardware building blocks built on top of those circuits — CPU, memory, storage, GPU, motherboard, PSU, cooling — the parts list of any computer, general-purpose or embedded.
