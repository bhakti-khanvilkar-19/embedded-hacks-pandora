Electronics

Every other domain in this notebook eventually rests on a handful of circuit-level ideas: what voltage, current, and resistance actually are; how resistors, capacitors, and inductors behave; how transistors switch; and how the analog world gets turned into numbers a microcontroller can use. This is that foundation — kept separate from Sensors (specific sensing elements) and Embedded (the microcontroller itself) because it's the layer underneath both.
